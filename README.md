💧 Smart Water Purification Bottle
A miniature water purification bottle with real-time water quality monitoring, designed to give underprivileged and slum communities access to safe drinking water.

Developed for the EPD (Engineering Product Design) module at General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University (KDU).

1758621257213
🎯 Problem
Many low-income and slum communities lack reliable access to clean drinking water, which leads to waterborne diseases and poor hygiene.

💡 Solution
A compact, affordable bottle that filters water through six layers and displays the purified water's TDS (Total Dissolved Solids) level so users can see its quality.
